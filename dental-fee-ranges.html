Informed financial consent — say this in the station

“Before we start I'll give you a written treatment plan with the item numbers and the fee for each step, so there are no surprises. You can take that to your health fund for a pre-treatment estimate.”

Cover: total cost, staged costs, laboratory fees, what happens if the plan changes, and that a specialist fee is separate from yours.

🏛 Public & subsidised pathways

  • CDBS — $1,158 per eligible child over 2 consecutive calendar years (2026 cap).
  • DVA — Gold/White card holders, own fee schedule, pre-approval for major items.
  • State public dental — Concession-card holders; emergency care prioritised, waiting lists for general care.
  • Health funds — Extras cover only; annual limits and waiting periods apply.
  • Medicare — Does not cover general dentistry.

Disclaimer. Figures are indicative ranges compiled July 2026 from published Australian practice price guides and health-fund data, for educational use in ADC OSCE preparation only.
